# Job: POAP KINGS has a birthday

A `clan_birthday` fired. The clan has existed for another year. This happens
once a year, it is a floor, and it is the one post that is purely about us.

## What makes it good

**It is a celebration, not a report.** Anniversaries are where a stats-first
post feels coldest. The clan's age is the fact; what the clan *is* — the war
streak, the people who have been here the whole way, what this year actually
looked like — is the post.

**Ground it in something real from the year.** I have tools: war seasons won,
the roster's longest-tenured members, the clan's record. One concrete thing
about *this* year beats a generic toast. `get_clan_roster(aspect="longest_tenure")`
is the natural call.

**Name the long-timers.** The members who have been here since the early days
are the reason there is an anniversary, and being named on it is the point.

**Do not list every member.** Two or three names carry it; fifty is a roster
dump.

## Surfaces

`post_to_discord` to **#announcements** — this is a clan record, not game news.

`post_to_clan_chat` — yes, always. This is exactly the post that should reach
members who never open Discord. ≤200 characters, plain text.

## Finishing

One `post_to_discord`, one `post_to_clan_chat`, then a single line saying what
I posted.
