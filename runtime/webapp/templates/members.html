How Elder works

Written for members. Every number below is read live from the engine constants and today’s roster — if the rules change, this changes with them.

Elder is participation, not prestige. Every input is something you control by playing. None of it rewards trophies, card levels or how long you’ve been here.

1. Hard filters — who is even considered

  • {{ elder.tenure_min }}+ days in the clan.
  • Still playing competitively, war or ranked: at least {{ elder.war_days }} war day with a deck played in the last {{ elder.war_window }} days, or {{ elder.ranked_battles }}+ ranked battles in the last {{ elder.war_window }}.

2. The score — three things, ranked against the clan

  • War rate — decks used ÷ decks available, last {{ elder.war_rate_window }} days.
  • Ranked battles played, last {{ elder.war_rate_window }} days.
  • Donations, averaged over four weeks so one big week doesn’t carry you and one quiet week doesn’t sink you.

War counts for {{ elder.war_weight }}% and donations {{ elder.donation_weight }}%. Ranked fills part of the gap war leaves, so doing both is worth more than either alone. Ranked counts participation, not the league you reach — a strong account doesn’t earn Elder by sitting on an old climb.

3. The band — how many Elders there are

Elders should be {{ elder.floor_pct }}–{{ elder.ceil_pct }}% of the clan. Right now that is {{ elder.floor }}–{{ elder.ceil }} of {{ elder.roster }} members, and we have {{ elder.elders }}.

  • The clan aims at the middle of that range ({{ elder.target }}), not the bottom.
  • Two people too close to separate can both get in, up to {{ elder.ceil }}.
  • If nobody has earned it, the count simply sits lower. Nobody is promoted to hit a number.
  • Being past the target is safe. You only lose the badge by stopping playing, or by being clearly out-ranked — and a near-tie always keeps the incumbent.

4. Sustained, not a snapshot

Qualifying once does nothing. It takes {{ elder.qualifying_weeks }} qualifying weeks in a row before a promotion even reaches leadership, who make the call. Going the other way, walking away from war and ranked entirely costs the badge in {{ elder.demote_weeks }} weeks; being out-ranked takes {{ elder.qualifying_weeks }}, so a swap lands as one move.

Short version: play war, play ranked, donate steadily, and keep doing it for a month. That is the whole thing.
